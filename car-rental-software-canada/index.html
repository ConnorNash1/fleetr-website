The Canadian details, handled

Sales tax by branch

Each branch charges its own sales taxes, HST or GST and PST, and they print correctly on agreements, receipts and invoices.

Kilometres and litres

Set your company's units once. Tank sizes, fuel prices, odometers and maintenance intervals all follow them.

Every time zone

Pickup and return times are kept in the branch's own time zone, including Newfoundland Standard Time.

Canadian plates and licenses

Provinces and territories on plates and licenses, and license checks that match how Canadian licenses read.

Canadian insurance work

Prices in dollars

Whole-dollar rates, Canadian dollars throughout, and card payments through Stripe in CAD.

Built in Atlantic Canada

fleetr was built in Newfoundland and Labrador, around the way rentals work in Atlantic Canada, where most are local: insurance replacements, a car while yours is in the shop, a weekend in Gros Morne or on the Cabot Trail. Questions people ask

Does fleetr charge HST correctly in every province?

Each branch is set up with the sales taxes it charges, so a branch in Nova Scotia and one in Ontario each apply their own rates on every rental.

Can I use miles instead of kilometres?

Yes. Units are a company setting, and figures already on file are converted for display, never changed.

Is it only for Atlantic Canada?

No. It works anywhere in Canada. It started in Atlantic Canada, and that's where fleetr.ai's renter pages are live first.
