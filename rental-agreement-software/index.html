Why companies drop paper

Paper agreements fail in the same few ways: a missing initial on the coverage box, a damage diagram nobody can read, a fuel level written as "3/4?" and a filing cabinet nobody can search when the claim arrives three weeks later. Each of those costs money when a renter disputes a charge.

With fleetr, the agreement can't be finished with a field missing, the photos prove what the car looked like, and any agreement is found in seconds by name, plate or code. Read our guide to replacing paper rental agreements for a step-by-step switch.

Questions people ask

Are electronic rental agreements legally valid in Canada?

Every province has electronic commerce legislation that recognizes electronic signatures and records. Have your lawyer review your rental terms either way; fleetr records who signed, what they saw and when.

What if the renter doesn't have a smartphone?

Your agent can run the same check-in on a tablet at the counter, and the renter signs there.

Can I change my terms?

Yes. You set your own terms and acknowledgements, and each agreement keeps the version the renter signed.

Can renters get a copy?

Yes. The agreement is available to the renter from their rental link, and receipts go by text or email.
