Decisions, not spreadsheets

Fleet management at a rental company comes down to a few questions asked every day: do we have the cars for tomorrow's pickups, which ones are earning, and which are costing us money? fleetr answers them from the rentals you already run, so there is nothing extra to keep up to date.

Questions people ask

Does fleetr track maintenance?

Yes. Each vehicle has a service interval in kilometres. When it's due, fleetr flags it and moves it to maintenance once it's next back, until someone marks the service done.

Can I see availability for a holiday weekend months ahead?

Yes. Availability looks a full year ahead by class, with sold-out and running-low dates called out.

Does it handle more than one branch?

Yes. Each branch has its own fleet, and executives can see one branch or the whole company.

Can it tell me when to sell a car?

It shows each car's depreciation, monthly value loss and net income, which is what that decision rests on.
