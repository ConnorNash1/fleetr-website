The whole rental, in order

Every step leaves a record, so nothing depends on someone remembering.

  1. BookedOnline on fleetr.ai, by phone or at the counter. The rate fills itself in from the class and where the booking came from.
  2. ConfirmedThe customer gets a text with their booking, what to bring and a link to check in.
  3. Picked upThey scan their license, photograph the car, choose coverages and sign on their phone. You hand over the keys.
  4. OutReturn reminders go out the day before. Extensions update the agreement and the charges.
  5. ReturnedThe customer logs the return, the time is locked in, and fuel and kilometres are compared with pickup.
  6. ClosedCharges are worked out, the card on file is charged or an invoice is sent, and any damage becomes a claim with photos.

Questions people ask

What is car rental software?

It's the system a rental company runs on: reservations, rental agreements, check-in and return, vehicle status, payments and reports. fleetr also lists your fleet for renters on fleetr.ai.

How long does it take to set up?

A guided setup walks through your branches, classes, rates, coverages and fleet, and you can take a test booking as soon as it's done.

Does it work on a tablet or phone?

Yes. The staff app works in any modern browser on a computer, tablet or phone, and customers use their own phone for check-in and return.

Can I take payments through it?

Yes. Cards are taken through Stripe on your own account: save a card at booking, hold the estimate at pickup and charge at close, or send a payment link.
