How independent rental companies get bookings online

Big brands spend heavily on advertising. Independents can win local bookings for far less, by being easy to find, easy to trust and easy to book.

By the fleetr team. November 17, 2026.

Your Google Business Profile

For "car rental near me" and "car rental" plus your city, Google's map results are often the first thing renters see. A complete Google Business Profile is free and is the highest-value hour you can spend:

  • The right business category, your exact address and a phone number that's answered.
  • Accurate hours, including holidays. Nothing loses a renter faster than a closed door during "open" hours.
  • Real photos of your cars, your lot and your counter.
  • A link to where renters can book.

Reviews

Renters choose between local companies largely on reviews. Ask every happy renter for one, ideally by text right after the return, with a direct link. Reply to every review, good and bad, politely and quickly; future renters read your replies. A few honest replies to complaints build more trust than a perfect score.

List where renters search

Renters compare companies on marketplaces before they book. Listing your fleet on one puts you in front of renters searching your city and dates, without building a booking engine yourself. On fleetr.ai, renters search by city and dates, see your real availability, rates and requirements, and book; the booking lands in your reservations with a verified phone number, and renters check in on their phone at pickup. See the city pages for St. John's, Halifax, Moncton, Charlottetown and Fredericton.

A simple website

You don't need a big site. You need one that loads fast on a phone and answers what renters ask: where you are, your hours, your classes and prices from, what to bring, your minimum age and deposit, and how to book. Put your city and "car rental" in the page title and first heading. Link to your listing so renters can book in one tap.

Answer fast

Same-day renters call several companies and book with the first one that answers. Answer the phone, reply to messages within minutes, and keep a few cars free for walk-ins and same-day calls when you can. The companies that answer fastest win the bookings the others never knew they missed.

Partners who send renters

Online bookings aren't only from renters. Body shops, dealerships and insurance adjusters book replacement cars every day. Give them an easy way to book online with the claim or repair order number, and they'll come back. Our guide to insurance replacement rentals covers winning that work.

Measure what works

Record where every booking came from: walk-in, phone, your website, a marketplace, a body shop, an insurer, a corporate account. Once a month, look at bookings and revenue by source. You'll quickly see which efforts pay and which don't.

Your Google Business Profile shows how many people found you, called or asked for directions, and the searches they used. If calls are high but bookings low, the problem is at your end of the phone. If profile views are low, work on photos, reviews and your categories. Ask walk-in renters how they heard of you; the answer is often a body shop or a friend, which tells you where to spend your time.

Give each change a month before judging it, and change one thing at a time, so you know what made the difference.

Questions people ask

How can a small car rental company compete with big brands online?

Be easy to find locally (a complete Google Business Profile), easy to trust (reviews and replies) and easy to book (a marketplace listing and fast answers).

Does a car rental company need its own booking website?

Not necessarily. Listing on a marketplace such as fleetr.ai lets renters book your cars online without you building a booking engine.

How do I get more reviews for my rental company?

Ask every happy renter, ideally by text right after the return with a direct link, and reply to every review.
