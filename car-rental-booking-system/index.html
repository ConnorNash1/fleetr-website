How a renter books

No booking engine to configure, no website to maintain.

  1. They searchBy city, address or airport code, with their dates. fleetr.ai shows companies with cars free for those dates.
  2. They chooseA class, with your daily rate, the total and your coverages, before tax. Your hours, minimum age and payment methods are on the page.
  3. They verify their phoneA text code confirms the number, so your bookings come from real people you can reach.
  4. You get the bookingIt's in your reservations straight away, and they get a confirmation text with a check-in link and a link to cancel.

Questions people ask

Do I need my own website to take online bookings?

No. Your branch gets its own page on fleetr.ai with your cars, rates, hours and requirements, and renters book there.

Do renters pay when they book?

Not by default. Renters reserve without paying and pay at pickup. You can ask them to save a card for pickup if your company takes saved cards.

Can I stop taking online bookings for a while?

Yes. Your listing can be switched off, and each class can be taken off sale on its own.

How do I avoid double bookings?

fleetr.ai checks your live availability, including cars out and due back, at the moment of booking.
