What to know before you drive in Charlottetown

The whole Island is close. Cavendish and the North Shore beaches are about 40 minutes away, and nowhere on the Island is more than a few hours' drive.

Leaving the Island. The Confederation Bridge to New Brunswick is about 45 minutes west of Charlottetown. There's a toll when you leave the Island, not when you arrive. Check that your rental company allows travel off the Island.

Rural roads. Many scenic roads are narrow, and some are red clay. Take your time, and watch for cyclists in summer.

Bring your license and a card. You'll need a valid driver's license, and most companies take a credit card. Each listing shows its minimum age and the payment methods it takes.

Questions people ask

Do I need a car on Prince Edward Island?

For most visitors, yes. Public transit is limited outside Charlottetown and Summerside, and the beaches and villages are spread out.

Is there a toll on the Confederation Bridge?

Yes, paid when you leave the Island. Arriving is free.

How early should I book a car in Charlottetown in summer?

As early as you can. July and August are the busiest months, and local fleets fill up.
