Picking up in Fredericton

Fredericton International Airport (YFC)

The airport is about 15 km southeast of downtown, around 20 minutes by car. Each listing says whether the company offers airport pickup.

Downtown and the north side

The city sits on both banks of the Saint John River. Pick a company on the side you're staying on.

University terms

Demand rises around the start and end of university terms. Book early in late August, September and April.

Opening hours

Local branches keep their own hours. fleetr.ai only offers pickup and return times inside them.

What to know before you drive in Fredericton

Day trips. Kings Landing is about 30 minutes west, Saint John and the Bay of Fundy about an hour south, and Moncton about two hours east.

Winter driving. Fredericton gets plenty of snow from December to March. Ask for a car with winter tires; each company lists which of its cars have them.

Wildlife. Deer and moose cross highways outside the city, most often at dawn and dusk.

Bring your license and a card. You'll need a valid driver's license, and most companies take a credit card. Each listing shows its minimum age and the payment methods it takes.
