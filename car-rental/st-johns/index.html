Picking up in St. John's

Near St. John's International Airport (YYT)

The airport is about 10 to 15 minutes from downtown by car. Some companies meet you at the airport or nearby; each listing says whether it offers airport pickup.

Downtown and the east end

Companies in town are handy if you're staying downtown, near Water Street, or swapping cars while your own is being repaired.

Opening hours matter

Local branches keep their own hours, often closed or short on Sundays. Your pickup and return need to fall inside them; fleetr.ai only offers times that work.

Winter tires

From late fall to spring, ask for a car with winter tires. Each company lists which of its cars have them.

What to know before you drive in St. John's

Plan for the weather. St. John's weather changes fast, with fog, wind and snow from November into April. Leave extra time, and keep the headlights on in fog.

Watch for moose. Outside the city, especially on the Trans-Canada Highway and rural roads at dawn and dusk, moose are a real hazard. Slow down after dark.

Day trips. Signal Hill and Quidi Vidi are minutes from downtown, Cape Spear (the most easterly point in North America) is about 20 minutes away, and the Irish Loop and Witless Bay make a full day. Gros Morne is a long drive across the island, so plan an overnight.

Bring your license and a card. You'll need a valid driver's license, and most companies take a credit card for the rental and any deposit. Each listing shows its minimum age and the payment methods it takes.

Questions people ask

How old do you have to be to rent a car in St. John's?

It depends on the company. Many set the minimum at 21 or 25. Each listing on fleetr.ai shows its minimum age before you book.

Can I pick up a rental car at St. John's airport?

Some local companies offer airport pickup, and others are a short drive away. Each listing says whether it picks up at the airport.

Do I pay when I book?

No. Booking on fleetr.ai doesn't charge you. You pay the rental company at pickup, and you can cancel free before then.
