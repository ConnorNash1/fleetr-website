Picking up in Halifax

Halifax Stanfield International Airport (YHZ)

The airport is about 35 km from downtown, roughly 30 to 45 minutes by car. A local company near your hotel can be cheaper than renting at the airport; each listing says whether it offers airport pickup.

Downtown, the North End and Dartmouth

Companies across the harbour are close to the bridges and the ferry. Pick the side you're staying on to save the crossing.

Bridge tolls

The Macdonald and MacKay bridges between Halifax and Dartmouth charge a small toll each way. Ask the company how it's handled.

Opening hours

Local branches keep their own hours. fleetr.ai only offers pickup and return times inside them.

What to know before you drive in Halifax

Getting around. Downtown Halifax is compact and hilly, with paid parking on most streets. Highways 102 and 103 take you out of the city quickly for day trips.

Day trips. Peggy's Cove is about 45 minutes away, Lunenburg about an hour and a quarter, and the Annapolis Valley around an hour. The Cabot Trail on Cape Breton is a weekend, not a day.

Winter driving. From December to March, snow and freezing rain are common. Ask for a car with winter tires; each company lists which of its cars have them.

Bring your license and a card. You'll need a valid driver's license, and most companies take a credit card. Each listing shows its minimum age and the payment methods it takes.
