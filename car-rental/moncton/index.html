What to know before you drive in Moncton

Day trips. The Hopewell Rocks on the Bay of Fundy are about 40 minutes south; check the tide times before you go. Shediac and Parlee Beach are about 25 minutes east, and Fundy National Park is just over an hour.

Crossroads of the Maritimes. Moncton is a natural starting point: Halifax is about two and a half hours away, and the Confederation Bridge to Prince Edward Island about an hour.

Winter driving. Snow is regular from December to March. Ask for a car with winter tires; each company lists which of its cars have them.

Bring your license and a card. You'll need a valid driver's license, and most companies take a credit card. Each listing shows its minimum age and the payment methods it takes.

Questions people ask

Can I drive a Moncton rental car to Prince Edward Island?

Usually, yes, but check with the company first. The Confederation Bridge charges a toll when you leave the Island.

How far is Moncton airport from downtown?

About 10 km, around 15 minutes by car.

Do I need to pay when I book?

No. Reserve on fleetr.ai without paying and pay the company at pickup.
