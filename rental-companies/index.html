What changes on day one

Bookings come to you

Renters find and book your cars on fleetr.ai. Every booking lands in your reservations next to the walk-ins, phone calls and insurance replacements.

Check-in takes a minute

The customer scans their license, photographs the car and signs the agreement on their phone. Your agent hands over the keys.

Paperwork is gone

Every agreement is signed, stored and searchable. Damage photos, fuel and kilometres are on the rental, not on a clipboard.

Texts go out on their own

Confirmations, return reminders, overdue notices and no-show follow-ups, in your own wording.

You get paid

Save a card at booking, hold the estimate at pickup and charge the final total at close. Invoice insurers and body shops on terms.

You see the business

Utilisation, revenue, money owed and what each car earns against what it loses in value.

Who it's for

fleetr is built for independent and regional rental companies: a single branch with twenty cars, or several branches with a few hundred. It suits companies that rent to the public, take insurance replacement work from body shops and adjusters, and look after corporate accounts, all at once.

It was built in Atlantic Canada around the way rental counters actually work: the phone ringing, a body shop asking for a car today, a customer late for their flight, and a car that came back a quarter tank short.

Questions people ask

Do I have to stop using my current system on the first day?

No. Most companies move their fleet, rates and upcoming reservations in first, run a few rentals on fleetr, then switch over. We help you move the data.

Do renters need to download an app?

No. Check-in and return open in the phone's browser from a text link. Nothing to install.

Can I keep my own rates and coverages?

Yes. You set your rates by class and source, your seasons and discounts, and your own coverages with your own wording.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your fleet size and branches. Book a demo and we'll walk through pricing for your operation.
