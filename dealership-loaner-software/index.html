Let inventory earn until it sells

Used inventory and demonstrators lose value every month they sit on the lot. With fleetr, you can rent them out on fleetr.ai to renters searching nearby, with your own rates and rules, and take any vehicle off rent the moment it sells or needs work.

You don't need to become a rental company to do it: no counter, no extra staff, no rental website. Bookings, agreements, check-in and returns run the same way as your loaners.

Questions people ask

What is dealership loaner software?

It's how a dealership hands out and tracks courtesy cars and service loaners: who has which car, the signed agreement, its condition, fuel and kilometres, and when it's due back.

Can we use our own vehicles?

Yes. Add your loaners and any inventory you want to rent, and choose which are listed for public rental.

Do we need rental insurance?

Renting to the public usually needs coverage beyond a dealer policy. Talk to your broker; we're happy to point you in the right direction.

Can body shops and insurers book with us?

Yes. Partners can book replacement rentals in their own portal with claim or repair order numbers, and you invoice them on terms.
